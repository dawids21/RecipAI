## Full description (2 034 / 4000 characters)

```
RecipAI keeps the recipes you actually cook in one place, turns them into a plan for the week, and writes the shopping list for you.

Found a recipe on a blog? Paste the link. Got it as a screenshot, or a photo of a page from a cookbook? Send the picture. RecipAI reads the ingredients and the steps and saves them as a real, editable recipe — instead of another bookmark you never open again.

SAVE RECIPES
• Import from a web page link
• Import from a screenshot or a photo
• Share a link or an image straight from your browser or gallery into RecipAI
• Or simply write a recipe yourself
• Name, servings, source link, ingredients and step-by-step instructions
• Add your own photos to any recipe
• Group recipes into collections

PLAN THE WEEK
• Keep several plans side by side — weekday cooking, guests, holidays
• A week-by-week agenda showing what is cooked on which day
• Add a saved recipe, with the serving size you are actually cooking
• Or add a plain note when a day needs no recipe
• Give each plan a colour and hide the ones you are not looking at

ONE SHOPPING LIST
• Build a shopping list from a meal plan in a few taps
• Or add the ingredients of any single recipe to a list
• The same ingredient appearing in three recipes becomes one line with the quantities added up
• Add anything else by hand
• Tick items off as you walk round the shop

COOK TOGETHER
• Share a recipe, a collection, a meal plan or a shopping list by email invite
• The other person accepts before anything is shared — nothing lands in their app uninvited
• Editors can change what you shared; viewers can only read it
• Withdraw an invite or remove access whenever you like

Sign in with your Google account and everything you save syncs to your phone. Everything you create stays private to you unless you deliberately share it with someone.

RecipAI was built by one cook who was tired of scrolling through screenshots in a gallery, retyping recipes into a notes app, and standing in a shop wondering how much passata the week actually needs.
```
