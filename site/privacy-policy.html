RecipAI

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

This policy explains what data the RecipAI Android app and its server ("RecipAI", "we") collect, why, where it is kept and how you can delete it. RecipAI is run by the RecipAI developer, who is the controller of your data. Questions go to dawid.stasiak21@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account data. You sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication. Firebase receives your Google account's name, email address and profile picture. The RecipAI server uses your email address to identify you and stores it with the content you own and the sharing you set up.
  • Content you create. Recipes (name, serving size, source link, ingredients, steps and photos), collections, meal plans with their entries and notes, and shopping lists with their items.
  • Sharing data. When you share something, the email address you invite, the invite, and who has access to what.
  • Recipe import data. When you import a recipe, the text of the web page you are viewing or the photo you pick is sent to the server for extraction. It is not stored unless you save the resulting recipe.
  • Usage counts. How many recipes, lists, plans and imports you have, used to enforce per-account limits.
  • Server logs. Technical logs of requests, which may include your email address, used to run and fix the service.

RecipAI does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, does not access your location or contacts, and does not sell your data.

How we use it

Only to provide the app's features: signing you in, storing and syncing your content, sharing it with the people you choose, importing recipes, enforcing usage limits and keeping the service working. The legal basis is performing the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and running (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase Authentication — sign-in and your account identity.
  • Google Gemini API — receives the web page text or photo you import, to extract the recipe.
  • Amazon Web Services S3 (Frankfurt, EU) — stores recipe photos.
  • A virtual private server — runs the RecipAI server and its database.
  • People you share with — see the recipes, meal plans and shopping lists you share with them, and your email address on the invite.

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Data kept on your device

The app keeps your preferences, an offline copy of your shopping list items and a log file on your phone. The log file leaves the device only when you tap Send logs in Settings and choose where to send it. Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your content from the server immediately — see Delete your account.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, and complain to your data protection authority. Most of this you can do in the app; for the rest, email dawid.stasiak21@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

RecipAI is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
