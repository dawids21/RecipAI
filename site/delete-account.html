Delete your RecipAI account

RecipAI, by the RecipAI developer

In the app

  1. Open RecipAI and tap the menu in the top-right corner.
  2. Open Settings and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm, then confirm your Google account when asked.

Your data is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email dawid.stasiak21@gmail.com from the Google account you use in RecipAI, with the subject RecipAI account deletion. Your account and data are deleted within 30 days and you get a confirmation by email.

What is deleted

  • Every recipe you own, with its photos
  • Every collection, meal plan and shopping list you own, with their entries and items
  • Your access to things others shared with you, and invites you sent or received
  • Your usage counts and any limits set for your account
  • Your RecipAI sign-in account

What is kept

  • Recipes, meal plans and shopping lists other people own and shared with you stay with their owners. Meal plan entries other people made from your recipes stay as plain placeholders.
  • Data stored on your phone (preferences, offline shopping list items, logs) stays until you uninstall the app.

Deletion cannot be undone. Signing in again with the same Google account starts a new, empty account. See the privacy policy for more.
